# Platform decisions

Verified against primary Android documentation on 2026-09-28. These are implementation choices, not physical-watch measurements.

- Both apps start their foreground recorder from a visible Activity after runtime permission checks. Remote Start is rejected while the receiving app is hidden; the watch records locally and shows phone video unavailable/unconfirmed. Android's [foreground service types](https://developer.android.com/develop/background-work/services/fgs/service-types) require microphone/camera types and permissions, and impose while-in-use start restrictions. The apps declare those types; no boot/background camera workaround is attempted.
- A watch microphone foreground service owns the sensors and PCM writer. A non-reference-counted partial wake lock has a ten-minute timeout, renewed while capture is active and released after Stop. Activity recreation does not own acquisition. [Sensor background restrictions and sampling](https://developer.android.com/develop/sensors-and-location/sensors/sensors_overview) still require validation on each watch firmware, especially screen-off behavior and power management.
- Native [SensorEvent](https://developer.android.com/reference/android/hardware/SensorEvent) timestamps and values are retained. Requested sampling is 10,000 microseconds with no batching; measured rate, gaps, accuracy, sensor identity and capabilities are metadata. [Motion sensor definitions](https://developer.android.com/develop/sensors-and-location/sensors/sensors_motion) distinguish total acceleration, gravity and linear acceleration; no substitute is fabricated when a sensor is missing.
- AudioRecord requests mono PCM16 at 44.1 kHz, tries supported native rates, prefers advertised UNPROCESSED and records the VOICE_RECOGNITION fallback. Actual client/device capture configuration and BOOTTIME AudioTimestamp observations are recorded. The app writes unchanged PCM samples; known framework rate conversion causes reopening at the device rate or a recorded audio failure. Vendor processing remains unvalidated. References: [AudioRecord](https://developer.android.com/reference/android/media/AudioRecord), [AudioRecordingConfiguration](https://developer.android.com/reference/android/media/AudioRecordingConfiguration), [AudioSource](https://developer.android.com/reference/android/media/MediaRecorder.AudioSource), [AudioTimestamp](https://developer.android.com/reference/android/media/AudioTimestamp).
- Phone capture uses CameraX with a front camera, HD preference and available-quality fallback. MP4 replaces Apple's MOV container. Camera audio remains in its encoded track; it is not the watch WAV. Only actual CameraX Start/Finalize events change capture status, and finalized track formats/presentation timing are inspected. [CameraX video capture](https://developer.android.com/media/camera/camerax/video-capture) describes the native Recorder lifecycle.
- [Wear Data Layer](https://developer.android.com/training/wearables/data/overview) carries expiring commands and immutable closed-file assets between signed companions. Files are acknowledged only after byte count and SHA-256 validation. Google Play services and manufacturer phone pairing are prerequisites. Raw files are retained independently of transport state; no cloud account/service is added.

No device-specific implementation is selected solely from a model name. The same watch APK targets Samsung Galaxy Watch5, Google Pixel Watch 2 and OnePlus Watch 2. Runtime sensor/audio capability evidence determines availability and fallback. Hardware qualification is separate in VALIDATION.md.
